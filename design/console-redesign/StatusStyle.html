Status, four ways

The chip is judged in a list, not in a swatch, so each direction is shown both ways. Marks stay: the shape is the second carrier of meaning and roughly one man in twelve needs it. Everything around the shape is up for the change.

Three carriers of one signal at once — a stroke, a tint and coloured text — around eleven-pixel capitals tracked out to 0.06em. Capitals plus tracking is the tell. It is a judgement rather than a measurement, but it is the one this reformulation is made on: a reader scans by word shape, and capitals flatten every word to the same rectangle.
  • Every state is equally loud, so fifty criticals read as wallpaper and the two that matter do not surface.
  • A bordered box around a bordered glyph is a box inside a box.
  • A 4-pixel radius on a 20-pixel object reads as a legacy tag control rather than a chip.

Built only from steps that already exist. Twelve-pixel text at regular weight is a step of the type scale; the full radius is an allowed named step rather than a written number; the box is padding, never a declared height. Tinted fill with coloured text on the same role is a pair the contrast suite already measures at 4.5:1 in both themes. No token changes and no lint-rule changes. Dark needs one adjustment: the neutral tint is the page ground there, so a neutral pill would punch a hole in the card it sits on — it takes the hover ground instead.
