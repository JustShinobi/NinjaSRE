The pressed item answers first
Selected state, heading and breadcrumb change on the click. A small spinner on the item itself says the data is still coming. Nothing else on the page waits for the server to agree about which page you are on.
The region transitions, the shell does not
Only the area below the heading cross-fades, over 200 ms, into a skeleton the same height as the content. The sidebar, the top bar and the live chip keep their identity — they are the same elements, not repainted ones.
What it does today
Every filter, sort and update is a full document navigation: the whole frame goes white, the sidebar repaints, scroll position is lost, and an operator watching an incident unfold has to press reload to find out whether it moved.
